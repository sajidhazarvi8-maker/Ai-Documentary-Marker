🎬 AI Documentary Maker

Free mobile-first documentary editor · local prototype

1 · Documentary script

This starter version splits your script into scenes. It does not yet invent or generate AI footage automatically.

2 · Scene visuals

Add one image or video per scene. Images are animated with a slow cinematic zoom. Your selected media stays on your device.

Create a scene plan to begin.

3 · Sound and style

Add a recorded narration and music track for a complete audio mix. This prototype does not synthesize a downloadable voice track yet.

4 · Preview and export

Export uses your browser's video recorder. Keep this page open while rendering. For best results use Chrome and keep the screen awake. Export support depends on your Android browser.

What this free MVP can and cannot do

✓ Script-to-scenes · image/video uploads · animated captions · cinematic colour filters · fade transitions · voice/music track mixing · MP4/WebM browser export where supported.

Not included yet: automatic AI image/video generation, automatic script writing, automatic voice generation, guaranteed MP4 on every phone, or unlimited free cloud rendering. These require additional models/services or a later server component.

Prototype · Your media is processed in your browser and is not uploaded by this app.
